## What is PluginLive?

PluginLive is an **all-in-one technology platform** that acts as a **virtual campus placement partner**. It connects **corporates, educational institutions, students, and assessment & training partners** to streamline and automate end-to-end campus placement and hiring processes.

---

- Provides tools like **mock interview platforms**, **resume builders**, and **skill assessment tests**
- Offers assessment opportunities to guide students toward career success
- Comprehensive support and resources for campus placements

### 2. Experienced Professionals (Lateral Hiring)
- Elevates career trajectories with solutions matched to expertise and goals
- Advanced lateral applicant tracking system that aligns skills with tailored job prospects

### 3. Colleges & Educational Institutions
- Simplifies and digitizes the campus placement process
- Competitive campus recruitment events

### 4. Corporates & Recruiters
- Discover and recruit top-tier young talent efficiently
- Targeted campus recruitment strategies
- Tailored talent management solutions
- Access to cutting-edge pre-employment assessments
- Lateral ATS technology for efficient candidate evaluation

## How It Works

```
Colleges onboard → Students take assessments → Scores & reports generated
                                                       ↓
Corporates post opportunities → ATS matches candidates → Hiring happens
```

1. **Colleges** register on the platform and manage their placement processes
2. **Students** take various assessments (communication, aptitude, coding, etc.)
3. **Assessment results** generate detailed reports with AI-driven insights
4. **Corporates** use the ATS to find, evaluate, and recruit matching candidates
5. **Platform** provides analytics and tracking throughout the entire pipeline

## Tech Stack (High Level)
- **Frontend:** React.js
- **Backend:** Node.js (Express)
- **Database:** PostgreSQL (via Prisma ORM)
- **Cloud Storage:** Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI)
- **Media Processing:** FFmpeg, Cloudinary
- **AI/ML:** Python (FastAPI) for assessment scoring and biometric verification
