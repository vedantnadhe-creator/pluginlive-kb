Communication Assessment

Candidate instructions

Please read these instructions carefully before beginning. This assessment evaluates your reading, listening, speaking and writing skills.

Before you begin

  • Complete the assessment in one sitting, before the stated deadline.
  • Use a laptop or desktop with a working camera, microphone and speaker or headphones.
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection and that your device is charged or connected to power.
  • Choose a quiet, well-lit location. Your face must remain clearly visible to the camera.
  • Allow camera and microphone permissions when requested.

What to expect

The assessment may include reading aloud, listening questions, video responses, written responses, dictation, sentence completion and sentence-building activities. Follow the on-screen instructions for each section carefully.

Do

  • Speak clearly and naturally during recorded tasks.
  • Read every question fully before answering.
  • Use correct grammar, spelling, punctuation and professional language in written responses.
  • Remain in full-screen mode and keep your face visible throughout.
  • Manage your time carefully; timers may apply to questions or sections.
  • Review responses where the platform permits it, then submit when all sections are complete.

Do not

  • Switch tabs, exit full-screen mode or open other applications.
  • Copy, paste, right-click, take screenshots or make screen recordings.
  • Use notes, websites, AI tools, translators, search engines or help from another person.
  • Use a mobile phone or any other device during the assessment.
  • Leave your seat or move out of camera view.
  • Refresh, close or restart the browser after the assessment begins.

Important

  • Your activity may be monitored to maintain assessment integrity.
  • Repeated violations, leaving full-screen mode or using prohibited resources may lead to automatic submission or disqualification.
  • Once an answer or section is submitted, you may not be able to return to it.
  • If you experience a genuine technical issue before starting, contact the assessment coordinator immediately. Do not try to resolve it by refreshing the page or changing devices after you have started.
